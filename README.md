## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
```
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.
```

## Proposed Solution
```
1. My app name is "FarmLoop".It is a fair-value agricultural exchange and direct distribution platform designed to reduce unnecessary intermediaries in the buying and selling process.My system is designed for multi-farmer Barter system.By exchanging the products farmers recieve credit points . He can use the credit point for future exchanges.this mechanism tracks the credits.In addition to barter exchanges, FarmLoop provides a direct marketplace for consumers and local buyers, along with group purchasing to consolidate demand.This FarmLoop app ensures benefits for both farmers and consumers.This does not remove the intermediate completely but reduce the intermediates.This app uses only one intermediate which makes a appropriate fair cost for the consumer.
```
